FAQ

Do I need to install anything on the robot?

No. The app is a ROS 2 node that joins the DDS network directly. Robots that use Zenoh only need their usual Zenoh router. To draw robot meshes on the phone, serve them over HTTP from the robot (Visualize your robot in 3D).

Which ROS 2 versions work?

Foxy, Humble, Jazzy, Kilted and Lyrical, with Fast DDS, Cyclone DDS or Zenoh, or any middleware through foxglove_bridge. See Requirements.

Is there a PC version?

Yes, for Linux (Ubuntu 20.04 or newer) and Windows (10 or 11), with the same tabs: The PC app.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet: Linux and Windows only, on x86-64 PCs.

Does it work over 4G or a VPN?

Yes, with a Discovery Server that both the phone and the robot can reach, with Discovery peers through the VPN, or with a Zenoh router or foxglove_bridge the phone can reach.

Is my robot’s data sent anywhere?

No. ROS 2 data goes straight between your phone or PC and your robots. See Privacy.

Can I use it on a tablet?

Yes, in portrait or landscape.

Can I use a gamepad?

On the phone, yes: Bluetooth or USB, in the Teleop tabs. On the PC, drive with the keyboard instead: W/S or the up and down arrows to go forward and back, A/D or the left and right arrows to turn, Space to stop.

Does the demo touch my network?

No: it uses its own domain (231) and only the device’s internal (loopback) network, on the phone and on the PC alike.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. An account is optional and only syncs your robot profiles across devices: Your account (optional).

Is it free?

Yes, with no ads. If it’s useful to you, you can support the project on GitHub Sponsors.
