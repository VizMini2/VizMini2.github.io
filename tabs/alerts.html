Alerts

Get a notification when something goes wrong on the robot, even with the app in the background.

  • Rules on any topic: a field above or below a threshold (/battery_state percentage < 0.2, a temperature, an error code) or no messages for a number of seconds (/scan stopped). Pick the topic and the field from the network, or type them; each rule is a warning or an error.

  • Each rule shows whether it is firing now and the last value. A rule notifies when it starts to hold and again when it recovers, not on every message.

  • Latest notices keeps the history of every alert: your rules, diagnostics turning ERROR and losing contact with the robot.

On the phone the switch at the top turns alert notifications on (they are off until you do); with the connection service (Configuration) they keep coming with the app in the background. On the PC they are desktop notifications while VizMini2 is open.
