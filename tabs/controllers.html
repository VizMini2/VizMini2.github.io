Controllers

The ros2_control controllers of a robot, like ros2 control: see them, switch them and move their joints.

  • Manager picks the controller manager (the ones on the network, or type its name; the default is /controller_manager).

  • Each controller shows its state (active, inactive, unconfigured), its type and rate, and the buttons its state allows: Activate, Deactivate, Configure and Unload. Interfaces lists the command and state interfaces it claims or needs.

  • Activating a controller that needs the joints of an active one offers to switch both in one go (deactivate the other and activate this one).

  • Declared but not loaded lists the controllers in the manager’s parameters that are not loaded yet, with Load.

  • Move joints (trajectory controllers and forward command controllers): one slider per joint, with the robot’s limits from robot_description and its current position from joint_states; Send, or Send while dragging. Trajectories take the time you set.

  • Hardware lists the hardware components with their state and interfaces, and activates or deactivates them.

It works with the controller managers of Foxy, Humble, Jazzy and Kilted/Lyrical (their messages differ: the version is detected on its own, or set it at the bottom). The demo robot has one too: deactivating its diff_drive_controller stops it.
