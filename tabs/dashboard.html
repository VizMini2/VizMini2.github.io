Dashboard

Your own screen, built from other tabs as widgets: a 3D view, several cameras, a joystick, buttons, diagnostics… all at once. The phone and the PC use the same dashboard.

  • Tap Edit, then Widget, and choose any tab type (except another dashboard). Tap Done to use the widgets again.

  • Widgets stay exactly where you drop them, and they can overlap: the one you moved last is on top, and a widget that covers another is drawn see-through with a colored border (a joystick over the 3D view, a small camera in a corner…). The board is 12 cells wide at 100 % zoom and grows to the right and down as you place widgets. The smallest widget is 3 × 2 cells.

  • In edit mode, drag a widget to move it, drag the round button at its bottom-right corner to resize it, or tap the red trash button to remove it. Undo (the arrow in the bar) reverts the last move, resize or removal; a removed widget comes back with its settings.

  • On the phone, in edit mode, Buttons, Sliders and Indicators widgets also show a + to add a button, slider or indicator to them (inside a dashboard they hide their own “Add” bar). An empty one also adds when you tap it.

  • Zoom: pinch with two fingers to zoom the board and drag with two fingers to move around it (two-finger gestures on a joystick belong to the joystick). The percentage in the bar takes it back to 100 %. The zoom is remembered for each dashboard on each device.

  • The pause button in the bar freezes every widget at once (messages keep arriving and the latest is shown on resume). While a bag plays from the Bags tab, its time bar appears here and pausing, changing the speed or jumping moves all the widgets together.

Each widget keeps its own settings, just like a normal tab.

On the PC, zoom with Ctrl + wheel and use Ctrl+0 to go back to 100 %, Ctrl+E to enter or leave edit mode, Esc to leave it and Ctrl+Z to undo. Right click a widget for Bring to front, Edit and Remove widget without entering edit mode.

Dragging topics in

Drag a topic from a Topics widget (on the phone, long-press it first), or a field of its message, and drop it on the dashboard:

  • on an empty spot, it becomes a new widget there: an image becomes a Camera, something RViz draws a 3D view, diagnostics a Diagnostics widget and a number a Plot;

  • on an RViz widget it adds a display, and on a Plot widget a series (if you dropped a whole topic, the series editor opens to choose the field).

Ready-made dashboards

The + screen has Ready-made dashboards that add a dashboard with its widgets already placed and configured, using the topics found on the network when there are any: Navigation (3D with map, laser, plan and footprint, joystick, speed and diagnostics), Camera robot, Monitoring (diagnostics, plots, indicators, node graph and TF tree), Manipulation (MoveIt) and Showcase (almost everything the app can do, made for tools/fake_robot.py). Demo mode also includes a Demo dashboard.

Panel layouts (.json) can be imported as a dashboard, and a dashboard can be exported as a panel layout: see Import and export.
