Bags

Record topics to MCAP files and play them back.

Record

Tap Record, choose the topics (or All) and tap Record. While it records you see the time, the messages and the size, topic by topic (and how many were dropped, if any), updated live. Tap Stop when you’re done. Recording and playback go on if you switch to another tab.

Files are saved as vizmini2_<date>_<time>.mcap (uncompressed, ROS 2 profile) and open in ros2 bag and in any tool that reads MCAP. Only topics whose type the app knows can be recorded; the others are not listed.

Play

Tap Play on a recording to publish its messages again at 0.5×, 1× or 2×, with loop and pause. The card shows the position and the length as it plays. Drag the progress bar to jump to any instant, also while paused. Jumping forward republishes the last message of latched topics (/tf_static, /map, /robot_description…) so the 3D view keeps the map and the robot. Topics whose type the app doesn’t know are skipped.

With Play only on this phone, the bag goes straight to the app’s tabs, without DDS (and the connection switches to an isolated one, so nothing from the network gets mixed in): the 3D view, cameras, plots, indicators… all see the same bag at the same moment, and nothing goes to the network or reaches the robot. Messages are delivered as they are in the bag, so even large clouds and images arrive whole, topics of types the app doesn’t bundle are not skipped, and plots use the bag’s time. While it plays on the phone, a timeline appears above the tabs, on every tab: pause, move to any moment or stop from there. Your connection comes back when playback stops.

Dashboards also show a playback bar while any bag plays, with pause, a slider to jump, the speed (tap it to go through 0.25× to 4×) and stop. To plot a bag without publishing anything at all, open it in a Plot tab.

Import and share

Import opens .mcap files, uncompressed or compressed with lz4 or zstd (the default of many ROS 2 setups). .db3 (SQLite) bags are not supported. Share sends a recording to another app (email, Drive…), and Delete removes it.

On the PC the recordings are in ~/.local/share/vizmini2/bags (Linux) or %LOCALAPPDATA%\VizMini2 Data\bags (Windows): Import opens a file dialog and Share shows where the file is. The Play only on this phone option works the same there, and the playback bar stays under whichever tab is open.

See Record and replay a bag.
