CameraMath

object CameraMath

Camera math (pure Kotlin, for the JVM): the intrinsics of a sensor_msgs/CameraInfo, the projection for Filament, the image corners and the points of a depth image.

ROS optical frame: z forward, x to the right, y down. The Filament camera looks towards -z with y up.
