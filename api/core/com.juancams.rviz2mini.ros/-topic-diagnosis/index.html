TopicDiagnosis

object TopicDiagnosis

Why messages from a topic are not arriving, based on what is known from the graph: no connection, nobody publishes it (and maybe there is one with a similar name, e.g. with a namespace), it is of another type, or there is a publisher but nothing arrives (almost always incompatible QoS: a best effort publisher with a reliable subscriber, or a latched topic without transient local). Each case may come with a concrete fix. No Android: tested on the JVM.
