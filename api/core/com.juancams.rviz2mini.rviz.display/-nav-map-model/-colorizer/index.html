Colorizer

class Colorizer(map: NavMapModel.NavMap, val layer: NavMapModel.Layer?, scheme: String, flat: Int, alpha: Float, smooth: Boolean)

The colors of a map's cells: by layer (null: flat, or the map's vertex colors if it has them and scheme is not "flat"), with scheme viridis, heat or rainbow over the layer's range of known values; U8 and log-odds layers as occupancy (free white, occupied black), as in navmap_rviz_plugin. Unknown cells: translucent grey. smooth: each vertex takes the mean of its known cells' values, so the colors blend across cells.
