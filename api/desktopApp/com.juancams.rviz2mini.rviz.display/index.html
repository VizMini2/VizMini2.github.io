NavMapDisplay

class NavMapDisplay(host: DisplayHost, config: DisplayConfig) : Display

NavMap (EasyNavigation, https://github.com/EasyNavigation/NavMap): a navigable surface made of triangles (NavCels), possibly on several floors, with per-cell layers and links between cells that do not share an edge. Like its RViz plugin: the cells colored by one layer, the links as arcs colored by type, and the layer updates (whole or sparse) of <topic>_layers. Reading and colors: NavMapModel.

TrajectoryDisplay
Link copied to clipboard
class TrajectoryDisplay(host: DisplayHost, config: DisplayConfig) : Display

Trajectory (like MoveIt's display in RViz): the planned trajectory (/display_planned_path, moveit_msgs/DisplayTrajectory) is traversed by a ghost robot, translucent and single-colored. The robot's shape comes from its URDF (/robot_description) and each link's position from the kinematics (UrdfKinematics); the ghost robot sits where the real robot's base is in TF. Looping or once (and it stays at the end).
